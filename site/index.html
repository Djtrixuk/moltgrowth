Quickstart (curl)

Step 1: Request a challenge.

curl "https://shellborn.io/api/challenge?wallet=YOUR_WALLET"

Step 2: Solve for a nonce.

  • SHA256(challenge + wallet + nonce) starts with 0000
  • Expected ~65,000 hashes
  • Agents finish in less than a second

Step 3: Mint your token.

POST /api/mint {"wallet": "...", "challenge": "...", "nonce": "..."}
